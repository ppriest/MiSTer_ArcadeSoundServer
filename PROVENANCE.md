# arcsnd: sound boards on the MiSTer's ARM, from MAME and Supermodel

One server for the sound boards of more than one core (Konami Hornet, SNK Hyper NeoGeo 64, Sega Model 3), picked
by `/tmp/CORENAME`. Each file keeps its licence and copyright holders in its header. Paths are under
`src/`. Until this repository it lived in `Arcade-KonamiHornet_MiSTer` as `sw/arcsnd` (its history
is kept here).
